Pitch Size Betting Insights — 2026

Big pitch, small returns.

We tested 25,738 matches across Premier League and Championship. Here's what actually matters for betting.

Key Finding

  • Large pitches = fewer goals and shots (-3.3% goals, -3.0% shots)
  • Home teams win fewer points on large pitches (-9.4%)
  • Corners are the real story: home teams get 22.8% more corners than away teams on large pitches

Betting Actions

Market Insight Action
Match Goals Large pitches suppress scoring Fade over 2.5 goals on 105x68+ pitches
Total Shots Fewer shots on big pitches Bet under shot lines when pitch is large
Corners Home teams dominate corners on large pitches Back home team corner advantage
Match Result Home points drop on large pitches Caution on home win bets; consider draws

Quick Rule

If a match is played on a 105x68 or larger pitch: - Expect fewer goals and fewer shots - Home corners are likely elevated - Home win probability may be slightly lower than expected

Confidence

  • High for corners (large effect, highly significant)
  • Medium for goals/shots (small effect, but significant)
  • Low for match result (no overall points difference)

Next Steps

  • Add pitch data to pre-match model
  • Track corner market movements closely
  • Test in-play when pitch conditions are confirmed
