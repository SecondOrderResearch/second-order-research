Founders

Second Order Research was founded by two practitioners who believe the same rigour used in institutional asset management can be applied to sports market analysis.

SW

Sean Westwood

CEO & Chief Sports Scientist

Sean spent the first half of his career inside the UK pension industry, building multi-asset investment frameworks and overseeing governance for defined-benefit schemes. That background gave him a deep respect for pre-registered hypothesis testing, correction for multiple comparisons, and the discipline of treating negative results as information rather than failure.

In the mid-2010s he shifted into football analytics, first as an investor in sports betting markets and later as a full-time researcher. He designed the Second Order methodology because he saw the same institutional blind spots in sports that he had seen in fixed income: people confuse narrative with edge, and they overreact to small samples. Sean leads the research programme, hypothesis registry, and external partnerships.

Outside the lab, he is a lifelong football coach and holds a postgraduate qualification in sports science.

RS

Richard Sorrell

CTO & Director of Research

Richard’s background is in fintech, where he built trading platforms, data pipelines, and real-time analytics systems for institutional clients. He brings experience in warehouse design, fault-tolerant ingestion, and the kind of operational discipline that turns raw data into decision-grade outputs.

At Second Order he owns the end-to-end data architecture: collection schedulers, cross-source normalisation, feature stores, and the statistical engine. He is the reason the research library can support rather than interrupt the hypothesis cycle, and he maintains the security and reproducibility standards inherited from regulated financial environments.

He lives in Fife, drives a Tesla Model 3 Performance, and still occasionally argues with spreadsheets at 2 a.m.
