Two further tests, both negative

The direction claim, tested without bearing data. We hold no pitch orientation or wind bearing, so a direct test is impossible. But the mechanism predicts something testable: if wind genuinely creates directional asymmetry, the variance of home_corners − away_corners must rise with wind, because wind direction relative to pitch varies randomly across fixtures.   • No gust data. Legacy files carry wind_speed_10m_max only. Today is a gust event (55–79 kph), which the sample never measured.
  • No direction. The one mechanism that would be tradeable — tailwind helping one end — needs hourly bearing, which the sample lacks.
  • No Scottish football. The entire sample is English Premier League. Today's worst-affected fixtures are in Scotland.
  • Only 17 of 241 matches exceed 40 kph. Today is beyond the 90th percentile of the data we hold — we are extrapolating, not interpolating.

A proper test needs the Open-Meteo archive backfilled with wind_gusts_10m_max and hourly direction per stadium across the full match history. That is the next build, not something I can responsibly claim today.

Today is the strongest wind event since this research began, so it is worth recording properly rather than letting it pass. A capture harness now logs every fixture with its kick-off-hour weather before results land, then fills in scores and match stats afterwards.

scripts/capture_wind_events.py writes to data/processed/wind_event_log.csv, keyed on (match_date, home, away) so re-runs update in place rather than duplicating. Three modes:

CommandWhenWhat it does
--date YYYY-MM-DDBefore kick-offLogs fixtures with wind, gust, bearing and 3h rain at the kick-off hour
--date ... --finaliseAfter ~19:00 UKFills final scores and result
--date ... --enrichNext dayFills corners, cards, fouls, shots from football-data.co.uk season CSVs

The enrichment path is verified end-to-end against a completed fixture (Leeds 1-1 Man United, 18 Oct 2026): corners 2/3, cards 2/2, fouls 16/5, shots 12/13 — all matching the source exactly. football-data.co.uk publishes weekly, so today's fixtures will only gain their stats on a later run. That is expected, not a failure.

What today can and cannot settle. One windy Saturday is a single data point, not a test — 90 fixtures with no control group tells us nothing on its own about whether wind mattered. Its value is cumulative: logged alongside every future windy card, with pre-match weather fixed before results are known, it becomes a genuinely out-of-sample test that avoids the post-hoc selection problem that sank the original claim. The log is only worth keeping if the weather is written before kick-off and never revised.

5 · The three things that would make this tradeable

  1. Backfill gusts, not just sustained speed. Open-Meteo archive returns wind_gusts_10m_max for any stadium coordinate back to 1940. The legacy 286-file sample only has sustained maxima — which is why today's gust event is outside its range. Partial run completed for 9 grounds before hitting Open-Meteo's hourly request cap; the resumable script is scripts/backfill_archive.py and needs multi-hour throttling or decade chunking.
  2. Add hourly wind bearing. This is the only version of the claim with a clean mechanism and a clean test: within a single match, the half played with a 60 kph crosswind should produce measurably different corner and shot counts than the half played against it. Paired design, opponent strength cancels out.
  3. Extend to Scotland. Every tradeable fixture today is Scottish or lower-English, and the sample has zero of either. The research library is currently generalising an EPL-only result to grounds it has never seen.
Method. Reproduce with python src/analysis/wind_analysis.py, which builds data/processed/wind_matched.csv from source. Legacy per-match Open-Meteo files (n=278) mapped to stadiums via nearest-coordinate match against data/reference/grounds.py, joined to data/processed/match_master_table.csv on date + home team → n=241 usable. Tests: Welch t-tests, Pearson correlation, likelihood-ratio tests against intercept-only logit, two-way ANOVA, Levene variance tests, 10,000-sample bootstrap, and a power calculation. Venue forecasts from Open-Meteo forecast endpoint at stadium coordinates, kick-off hour. Odds from BetMGM/DraftKings via SportsGambler, FanDuel and ESPN, captured 24 Oct 2026.

Caveat. Every price quoted is pre-match and will move. The wind claim in the research library does not replicate on the data we hold; the fouls/cards/second-half signal is a 29-match lead that fails Bonferroni correction. Treat the picks above as hypotheses with a mechanism, not as validated edges. Stake accordingly — or not at all.
