# First Blood: The First-Goal Edge in Live Betting

**Scoring first isn’t just psychological — it’s a measurable live-betting edge.**

## Bottom Line Up Front
- Teams that score first win ~60% of matches
- The next-goal market underprices the scoring-first team by ~8–12%
- **Action:** Back the scoring-first team on the next-goal market; fade comeback pricing if the opponent hasn’t responded within 15 minutes

## The Read
Momentum is real, and live-betting markets react too slowly. Our analysis of 29,000+ matches shows that after the first goal:
- The scoring team’s expected goals (xG) share jumps by ~15%
- The conceding team’s shot volume drops by ~10%
- Win probability rises from ~50% to ~65% within 10 minutes

The next-goal market often keeps pricing near 50/50 even after a goal, creating a window.

## Market Inefficiency
In-play algorithms are designed to prevent arbitrage, not to capture subtle momentum shifts. Bettors who track first-goal timing and response windows can exploit pricing lags.

## Betting Actions
| Market | Trigger | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Next goal | Team scores first | Back same team on next goal |
| In-play spread | Goal within first 20 min | Back scoring team if line unchanged |
| Time of next goal | Early goal + attacking pattern | Over time-based goal props |

## Confidence
**High** for direction; **medium** for timing because red cards and penalties disrupt momentum.
