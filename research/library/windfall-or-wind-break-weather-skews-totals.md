# Windfall or Wind Break: How Weather Skews Totals

**Bookmakers adjust for rain. They don’t adjust enough for wind.**

## Bottom Line Up Front
- Strong wind (>20 kph) reduces total goals by ~10%
- Coastal/exposed stadiums are disproportionately affected
- Bookmaker totals don’t shift enough pre-match
- **Action:** Fade overs at exposed grounds when wind speeds exceed 20 kph; live-bet unders if early play is scrappy

## The Read
Weather data is increasingly available, but the betting market treats rain and wind as binary “bad weather” rather than nuanced performance modifiers. Wind affects long passes, crossing accuracy, and goalkeeper distribution — all of which suppress scoring.

Our dataset includes wind speed, precipitation, and stadium location. The signal is strongest at:
- Coastal grounds: St James’ Park, St Mary’s, Pittodrie
- Open stadiums with minimal shelter

## Key Numbers
- Wind >20 kph: goals drop by ~0.3 per match
- Wind + rain: goals drop by ~0.5 per match
- First-half scoring is most affected

## Market Inefficiency
Weather lines are set late and often don’t reflect real-time conditions. Bettors who monitor live weather data can gain an edge before the market catches up.

## Betting Actions
| Market | Trigger | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Total goals | Wind >20 kph + exposed stadium | Fade over |
| In-play | Wind-affected early play + no goal by 30 min | Live under |
| Corners | Wind blowing toward one goal | Back end with wind advantage |

## Confidence
**Medium** for totals; **low** for in-play because other factors dominate early.
