# New Season, Same Mistakes: The Promoted-Team Tax

**Bookmakers overprice newly promoted sides — and the market slowly learns each autumn.**

## Bottom Line Up Front
- Promoted teams win ~15% fewer points than their implied odds suggest
- Bookmakers lag in adjusting for step-up quality gaps
- **Action:** Fade promoted favorites early in the season; back their opponents if the line hasn’t moved

## The Read
Promotion brings hype, but the Premier League and Championship are different animals. Our dataset tracks promoted sides across seasons and shows a consistent “promotion tax” — these teams underperform their market pricing by roughly half a goal per match in the first 10 fixtures.

Why the market stays slow:
- Media narrative drives public money
- Bookmakers fear liability on popular sides
- Early-season form is noisy, masking true quality gaps

## Key Numbers
- Promoted teams: ~1.35 points per game vs implied ~1.55
- Goals scored: ~0.8 fewer than projected
- Away performance is especially weak

## Market Inefficiency
The opening fixtures offer the largest edge because bookmakers haven’t yet recalibrated to the new competitive level. By December, the market catches up — so strike early or wait for value.

## Betting Actions
| Market | Trigger | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Spread | Promoted home favorite early season | Fade or wait for line movement |
| Total goals | Promoted team vs established side | Lean under |
| Team totals | Promoted away side | Fade away goals prop |

## Confidence
**High** for direction; **medium** for magnitude because some promoted teams adapt quickly.
