# Derby Day: When Rivals Play, Goals Disappear

**Local derbies are tighter, meaner, and lower scoring than the model expects.**

## Bottom Line Up Front
- Derby matches average ~0.8 fewer total goals than non-derbies
- Shot volume drops by ~12%
- Draws happen ~10% more often
- **Action:** Lean under on totals and Asian handicaps in derby fixtures; consider draw insurance

## The Read
Derby matches are different animals. The psychology, the history, the fear of losing to your neighbor — all of it compresses scoring. Our dataset flags local rivalries and shows consistent suppression of attacking metrics.

Derbies tend to feature:
- More fouls, fewer clear chances
- Conservative tactics from both sides
- Higher red card risk when emotions flare

## Market Inefficiency
Bookmakers don’t fully derate totals for rivalry fixtures. The over/under line is often set too high, creating value on the under.

## Betting Actions
| Market | Trigger | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Total goals | Derby fixture | Lean under 2.5 |
| Asian handicap | Home favorite in derby | Consider home -0.5 or draw hedge |
| Both teams to score | Derby | Lean no on BTTS |
| Cards | Derby | Over cards likely |

## Confidence
**High** for goal suppression; **medium** for exact magnitude because some derbies break the pattern.
